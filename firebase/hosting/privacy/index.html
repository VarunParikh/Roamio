How Roamio, the travel bill-splitting app, uses your data, and what you can do about it.

Adult notice

Version 2026-10 · For people aged 18 and over

Who we are

Roamio is an app for splitting travel costs with friends. It is run by Varun Parikh, who decides how your data is used (the “Data Fiduciary” under India’s DPDP Act). The pilot is small (about 10 people) and the app is not sold or used for advertising.

What we keep, and why

Your name, email and how you sign in (Google or email + password)To create your account and let you sign in.
That you confirmed you’re 18 or older (the sign-up tick)Roamio’s pilot is for adults. We don’t ask your date of birth; we record that you confirmed your age, and when.
Profile photo and short description (optional)So your squad recognises you.
Your profile currencyTo show amounts in money you know.
Your choices for notifications, location and cameraSo the app only does what you allowed.
A code that lets us send notifications to your phoneFor alerts when friends join and when you near your budget. Removed when a phone stops using it.
Your adventures, squads, roles, tabs (expenses), who paid, who shares, transfers you marked as settled, and photos added to tabs (“memories”)That’s what the app is for: splitting costs fairly. Your squad sees these.
Nicknames of “pals” (people on a trip without the app)To split costs with them. Nickname only; nothing else about them.
Your personal budget for an adventureTo warn you privately at 80% and 100%. Only you can see it.
Where a tab happened, if you turn Location on and add itTo remember the spot. Stored as a map point only, no address. Your squad can see it.
Invite codes you opened, and activity on your adventuresSo squads can see what changed and why.
A short audit record when your data is downloaded or an account is deletedTo prove we honoured your rights.

We don’t collect your phone number or contacts. Every photo is shrunk and its hidden details (including GPS location) are removed on your phone before it is uploaded. “Scan a bill” reads the bill on your phone; the image isn’t sent anywhere unless you attach it to the tab. Roamio has no ads and, today, no analytics or crash reporting in the app.

Where it’s kept

On Google’s Firebase service, in the Mumbai (asia-south1) region in India. Data is encrypted while it travels and while stored. Security rules on the server — not just the app screens — decide who can read what.

Who else is involved

Google FirebaseRuns the app and holds the data above, as our processor. If you sign in with Google, Google also confirms who you are.
Open-MeteoWeather on your Home card. Gets your approximate position (about 1 km) only if you turned Location on; otherwise just the adventure’s city and country.
Google Play IntegrityA check that the app on your phone is the real Roamio; it says nothing about what you do in the app (Firebase App Check).
UnsplashCountry photos for adventure banners. Our server asks for a photo of the country, never anything about you.
GitHubYour phone asks, at most once a day, whether a newer Roamio version exists.
ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com)Our server fetches the day’s exchange rates. Nothing about you is sent.
Email provider (via Firebase “Trigger Email”)Sends parental consent requests and account notices, so it gets the email address and message.

These services see your phone’s internet (IP) address when your phone talks to them. We don’t sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, so the app works.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, profile photos, budgets, settings, notification codes and sign-in are deleted straight away (always within 30 days). Tabs you were part of — including photos you added to them — stay with your squad, shown as “Deleted user”, so everyone’s balances stay right.
  • Emails we send (consent requests and account notices) are deleted from our outbox after 30 days.
  • Audit records are deleted after 1 year.
  • Parental consent records (if you are a parent) are kept for the life of the child’s account plus 1 year.

Your rights — in You → Privacy centre

  • Download your data: a file of everything we keep about you, plus your photos as zip files.
  • Correct your data: change your name, photo and description on the You page. To fix your date of birth, write to us.
  • Delete your account. If you lead a squad with others in it, make someone else leader first.
  • Nominate a person to use these rights for you if you can’t. During the pilot, write to our grievance officer with their name and email; choosing one in the app comes in Phase 2.
  • Change your mind: switch notifications, location or camera on or off at any time.
  • Raise a concern with our grievance officer. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong

If your data is ever exposed, we’ll tell you without delay — what happened, what it means for you, what we’re doing and what you can do — and report it to the Data Protection Board.

Changes

If we change this notice we’ll update its version and date, and tell you before a change that affects you takes effect.

Child notice (for parents and guardians)

Version 2026-10 · For the parent or lawful guardian of a 13–17-year-old

Not in use during the pilot. Roamio’s Android pilot is for adults (18 and over). This notice applies once under-18 accounts open in Phase 2.

Your child wants to use Roamio, an app for splitting travel costs with friends. Because they are under 18, Indian law (DPDP Act §9) needs your verifiable consent first. Roamio is run by Varun Parikh.

How consent works

  1. Your child enters your email. We email you a single-use link that works for 7 days (reminders on day 3 and 6).
  2. You sign in to Roamio — or create an account — with that email. Your account must show you are 18 or older. Falsely claiming to be a child’s parent or guardian is an offence.
  3. You read this notice and tap I give consent or Decline.
  4. We record your decision, this notice’s version, how you were checked and when. Your child can’t change this record.

If you decline, or nobody answers within 7 days, your child’s account and data are deleted and they are told by email.

What we keep about your child

  • Their name, email and date of birth. Until you say yes, this (plus your email and relationship) is all we keep.
  • After you say yes: an optional profile photo and description, their profile currency, notification and camera choices, and a notification code for their phone.
  • The adventures, tabs (expenses), photos and settled transfers they add or are part of — their squad sees these.
  • Their personal budget — private from the squad.
  • A short audit record when their data is downloaded or their account is deleted.

Extra protections for under-18s

  • No tracking or monitoring: no analytics and no crash reports on a child’s account. No ads.
  • No location: they can’t tag where a tab happened, and the weather uses the adventure’s city, not where they are.
  • Their budget stays private from the squad. You can see it only in a data download you request.
  • Photos are shrunk and stripped of hidden details (including GPS) on the phone before upload.
  • No phone number or contacts are collected.

Where it’s kept and who else is involved

On Google Firebase in Mumbai (asia-south1), India, encrypted in transit and at rest. The services involved are the same as for adults, except that Open-Meteo only ever gets the adventure’s city.

Your controls — You → Linked children and Privacy centre

  • See your child’s status and the date you gave consent.
  • Download your child’s data.
  • Ask for a correction by writing to the grievance officer.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Your child’s account is frozen straight away and deleted after 48 hours; they are told. Tabs they were part of stay with the squad as “Deleted user”.

Your child can also change their notification and camera choices, download their data and delete their account themselves.

How long we keep it

  • While the account is open.
  • When the account is deleted, their profile, profile photos, budgets and sign-in are deleted straight away (always within 30 days); tabs, including photos they added to them, stay with the squad as “Deleted user”.
  • Emails we sent about the request: deleted from our outbox after 30 days.
  • Your consent record: for the life of the child’s account plus 1 year.
  • Audit records: 1 year.

When your child turns 18

The link to you is removed, you are told, and they accept the adult notice themselves before using the app again.
